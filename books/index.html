FAQ

Questions about the books

Where can I buy the books?

All four books are published on Amazon through Kindle Direct Publishing. Click "Buy on Amazon" on any book to see the available formats and price in your country.

I'm a junior developer. Are these too advanced?

No. Every book is written to take you from junior to senior, so it starts with the fundamentals and builds up to the questions asked in senior interviews.

Which book should I read first?

Start with Kotlin if the language itself still feels shaky, or Core Android if you're preparing for your first Android role. Then move to Coroutines & Flow and Jetpack Compose, which come up in almost every modern Android interview.

How are the books different from the courses?

The books focus on interview questions and clear explanations. The courses are hands-on: you build complete apps from scratch. Many people use the books to prepare for interviews and the courses to level up their day-to-day skills.
